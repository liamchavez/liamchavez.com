California & Nevada — 11 days, 5 national parks
Newport Beach as a base, the Channel Islands by ferry, Death Valley in September heat, Sequoia and Kings Canyon back to back, Yosemite at the end before a 6am flight home from Anaheim. The drive through Tioga Pass at dusk was the best hour of the whole trip.
